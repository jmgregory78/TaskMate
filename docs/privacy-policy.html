Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

Overview

TaskMate ("we," "our," or "us") is a household task management app built to help families stay on top of recurring maintenance tasks and supplies. We take your privacy seriously and are committed to being transparent about how we handle your data.

What Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address and display name when you create an account
  • Household name and membership information

Task & Supply Data

  • Tasks, schedules, and recurrence settings you create
  • Supply items, quantities, and purchase history
  • Task completion history and activity logs

Device Information

  • Push notification token (to send you reminders)
  • Basic device information for app functionality

Usage Data

  • Feedback you voluntarily submit through the app

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Provide and improve the TaskMate service
  • Send you task reminders and notifications you have requested
  • Allow household members you have invited to collaborate
  • Respond to feedback you submit

We never:

  • ✓ Sell your data to third parties
  • ✓ Use your data for advertising
  • ✓ Share your information with anyone outside your household

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase, which is protected by industry-standard security measures. Data is stored in the United States.

Your Rights

You can at any time:

  • Edit or delete any task, supply, or household data within the app
  • Delete your account by contacting us
  • Request a copy of your data

Children's Privacy

TaskMate is designed for household management by adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
